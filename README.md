# Zelda Master Sword

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.2. A legendary sword lies deep in the Dark Forest,
planted in a stone pedestal and hidden by a fog that thickens as you get closer.
Pull the sword out and the fog lifts for good.

> Fan-made mod, inspired by *The Legend of Zelda*. It is not affiliated with or
> endorsed by Nintendo. It uses no assets from the games: the textures, models
> and structures were made for this mod.

## Features

### The sword

- **Netherite-level stats**: 8 attack damage, 1.6 attack speed, 2031
  durability. Epic rarity, fire resistant.
- **Every sword enchantment at once**: Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods
  can be stacked on the same sword, along with all the others. Use the
  enchanting table or the anvil.
- **Repaired only by another Master Sword**, in an anvil, a grindstone or the
  crafting grid. No ingot or other material repairs it.
- **It mends itself**: once it drops to half durability, it starts regaining
  durability on its own until it is whole again, at a pace that would restore a
  full bar in **2 in-game days**. Time spent asleep counts; fighting with it
  holds the mending back.
- **Beam of light**: a fully charged swing at full health sends a wave of light
  forward. It flies 12 blocks, passes through several mobs, deals 60 % of the
  sword's melee damage and costs 1 durability. 15-second cooldown.

### The shrine

- **Generated in the Dark Forest** only, and only where the whole shrine fits
  inside the biome, never on a river bank or at the forest edge. As rare as a
  woodland mansion, and never close to one.
- **A forest clearing** around a pedestal of stone bricks, with the sword
  already planted in it.
- **The guardian fog**: from 50 blocks away, a mist thickens around the shrine
  as you approach, down to about 12 blocks of visibility. It disappears **for
  good** once the sword is pulled out.
- **The pedestal**: right-click with an empty hand to draw the sword. Right-click
  with any sword to plant it back, which makes a nice display for your base (the
  fog does not come back). Breaking the pedestal takes a diamond pickaxe.
- **Advancement**: *Drawn from the Stone*.

### The cartographer's map

- **Every level 3 (Apprentice) cartographer** offers a **Sacred Grove Map**
  leading to the nearest shrine, marked with its own icon. It costs 14 emeralds
  and a compass, like the woodland explorer map.
- Shrines are rare: expect a trip of **around 5,000 blocks**. Each map points to
  a shrine no earlier map has claimed.

## Configuration

`config/mastersword.json` is created on first launch. It explains itself in a
`_comment` block at the top. `/mastersword reload` applies most values without
a restart.

| Section | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| `item` | `attack_damage`, `attack_speed`, `max_durability`, `full_regen_days`, `regen_starts_below`, `unbreakable`, `repairable_with_netherite_ingot` |
| `light_wave` | `enabled`, `cooldown_seconds`, `range`, `speed`, `damage_ratio`, `width`, `durability_cost`, `requires_full_health` |
| `fog` | `enabled`, `outer_radius`, `inner_radius`, `visibility`, `color` (client-side only) |

`attack_damage`, `attack_speed` and `repairable_with_netherite_ingot` need a
restart. Lowering `max_durability` below the damage a sword has already taken
loses that damage: the file explains why.

## Compatibility

- **Sodium**: the fog works.
- **Shader packs** (Iris): no fog while a shader pack is on, because the pack
  draws its own fog. Everything else works.

## Installation

Requirements:

- Minecraft **26.2**
- Fabric Loader **0.19.5** or newer
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- Java **25**

Install the mod on **both the client and the server**: the fog and the pedestal
are drawn by the client, and the shrine is generated by the server.

## Building

```bash
./gradlew build
```

The jar lands in `build/libs/`.
